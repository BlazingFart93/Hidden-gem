Hidden Gems helps you uncover unique and unforgettable food spots near you. Explore local favorites, hidden restaurants, and must-try dishes all near you.

Snocorner

Snocorner is a local spot known for refreshing shaved ice and sweet treats, offering a variety of flavorful options in a casual, laid-back setting. It’s a great hidden gem for anyone looking for a cool dessert in Long Beach.

Sushi Mafia

Sushi Mafia is a Long Beach sushi spot known for its creative signature rolls, fresh sushi, sashimi, and Japanese-inspired dishes. With unique menu options and a fun, welcoming atmosphere, it’s a great local choice for anyone looking to try something beyond the usual sushi.

La Michoacana

La Michoacana is a local dessert spot offering a variety of Mexican-style treats, including refreshing paletas, ice cream, aguas frescas, and other sweet snacks. It’s a great place to stop by when you’re craving something cold, colorful, and flavorful.
